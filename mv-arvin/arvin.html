Teaching note

When a crest sits amidships and the ends hang over troughs, the hull hogs: the deck goes into tension and the bottom shell into compression. Real hogging collapse usually starts as bottom-plate buckling, followed by tensile tearing of the deck.

The crumpled deck in Shot 2 follows the brief as a visual effect. Use it in class to ask students where the buckling should really start.
